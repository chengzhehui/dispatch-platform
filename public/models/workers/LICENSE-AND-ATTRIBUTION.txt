The application clones the skeleton per person, shares mesh/material assets,
normalizes height to approximately 1.85 metres and plays Idle, Walk, Run,
Run_Back, Run_Left and Run_Right clips. Flat-ground jumping adds a procedural
joint pose and vertical trajectory; the source asset has no dedicated jump clip.
Welding adds procedural arm, wrist and crouch rotations on the existing skeleton,
with simple arc/spark geometry generated by the application.
Material roughness and metalness are adjusted in memory for scene lighting.
The source GLB on disk has not been modified. No external runtime CDN is used.
